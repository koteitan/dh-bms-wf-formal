# P-09: Definition 11.1, the $\hat\Sigma_1$ form of admissibility

## The paper

### Statement

Let $\mathrm{KPAx}(d)$ be the $\Delta_0$ predicate expressing that $d$ is the code of an axiom
of $\mathrm{KP}$ or of an instance of an axiom schema of $\mathrm{KP}$.

Definition 11.1.

$$
\mathrm{KPTrue}(M,U,S) \ :\Longleftrightarrow\
\forall d \in \omega\ \bigl(\mathrm{KPAx}(d) \to \langle d,\emptyset\rangle \in S\bigr),
$$

$$
\mathrm{Adm}(\eta) \ :\Longleftrightarrow\
\exists M,c,U,S\ \bigl(\mathrm{LCode}(\eta,M,c) \wedge \mathrm{SatCode}(M,U,S) \wedge \mathrm{KPTrue}(M,U,S)\bigr).
$$

Hence $\mathrm{Adm}$ is a single strict $\hat\Sigma_1$ formula.

Lemma 11.2 (correctness of the admissibility form). If $\theta$ is admissible and
$\eta < \theta$, then

$$
L_\theta \models \mathrm{Adm}(\eta) \iff L_\eta \models \mathrm{KP}.
$$

### Proof

Definition 11.1 is a definition, so there is no proof. The proof of Lemma 11.2 is as follows.

Right to left is by Corollary 10.6 and the existence of a satisfaction code inside $L_\theta$.
Left to right is by $\Delta_0$ absoluteness of $\mathrm{LCode}$, $\mathrm{SatCode}$ and
$\mathrm{KPTrue}$, together with Lemmas 9.2 and 8.4.

## Lean

### The statement in Lean

Let $h$ be the set the codes live in ($h = L_\omega$), let $w$ be the set the variable indices
live in ($w = \omega$), and let $D$ be the domain.

$$
\mathrm{KPTrue}(M,U,S)\ :\Longleftrightarrow\
\forall d \in h\ \bigl(\mathrm{KPAx}(d) \to \langle d,\emptyset\rangle \in S\bigr),
$$

$$
\mathrm{Adm}^D(\eta)\ :\Longleftrightarrow\
\exists M,c,U,S \in D\ \bigl(\mathrm{LCode}(\eta,M,c) \wedge \mathrm{SatCode}(M,U,S) \wedge \mathrm{KPTrue}(M,U,S)\bigr).
$$

Here $\mathrm{KPAx}$ is the one from [P-04](P-04.md) and $\mathrm{LCode}$ the one from
[P-03](P-03.md).

There are two claims to prove.

**(1) Complexity.** $\mathrm{Adm}$ is a $\hat\Sigma_1$ predicate over the support
$\{h,w,x\}$.

**(2) Lemma 11.2.** For $\theta$ admissible and $\eta < \theta$,

$$
\mathrm{Adm}^{L_\theta}(\eta) \iff \eta \text{ is an admissible ordinal}.
$$

The right-hand side is $\mathrm{Adm}(\eta)$ of [P-04](P-04.md) (the three conditions), and its
equivalence with $L_\eta \models \mathrm{KP}$ is shown in that same section.

### The proof in Lean

#### (1) Complexity

$\mathrm{LCode}$ is $\Delta_0$ ([P-03](P-03.md)), $\mathrm{SatCode}$ is $\Delta_0$ (§8), and
$\mathrm{KPTrue}$ is $\Delta_0$. That $\mathrm{KPTrue}$ is $\Delta_0$ comes from
$\forall d \in h$ being bounded, from $\mathrm{KPAx}$ being $\Delta_0$ ([P-04](P-04.md)), and
from $\langle d,\emptyset\rangle \in S$ being $\Delta_0$. The last one is bounded by catching
$\emptyset$ inside the pair as "the set with no element".

Take the conjunction of the three, assign the variables $M,c,U,S$ and put four existential
quantifiers on top. A $\Delta_0$ formula with an unbounded existential block on it is
$\hat\Sigma_1$, so the result is $\hat\Sigma_1$. The support becomes $\{h,w,x\}$ ($x$ being
the variable for $\eta$).

Where the paper says in one line that "$\mathrm{Adm}$ is a single strict $\hat\Sigma_1$
formula", Lean assembles "a conjunction of three $\Delta_0$ predicates with four existential
quantifiers". To keep the alternating block shape
($\hat\Sigma_1 = \exists\vec x\,\delta$), the four existential quantifiers are collected into
one block.

#### (2) Lemma 11.2

**Direction $\Rightarrow$.** From $\mathrm{Adm}^{L_\theta}(\eta)$ take $M,c,U,S \in L_\theta$
satisfying the three conditions.

- Lemma 9.2 (soundness in [P-03](P-03.md)) gives $M = L_\eta$.
- From $\mathrm{SatCode}(L_\eta,U,S)$ and $\mathrm{PUCl}(U)$, every standard finite sequence
  with values in $L_\eta$ belongs to $U$ (Lemma 8.2). In particular the empty sequence
  $\emptyset \in U$.
- A code recognized by $\mathrm{KPAx}$ is a **sentence** (its free variables are empty). This
  comes from the clause "$\forall k \in w\ \mathrm{NotFree}(k,d)$" contained in the definition
  of $\mathrm{KPAx}$. Hence the empty assignment is an appropriate assignment.
- For each $\varphi$ with $\mathrm{KPAx}(\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner)$, $\mathrm{KPTrue}$ gives
  $\langle\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner,\emptyset\rangle \in S$. Correctness of the satisfaction
  code (Lemma 8.4) gives $L_\eta \models \varphi[\emptyset]$.
- This is exactly $L_\eta \models \mathrm{KP}$, so by the equivalence of [P-04](P-04.md)
  ($\mathrm{Adm} \iff \mathrm{KPModel}$), $\eta$ is an admissible ordinal.

Where the paper writes one line, "by $\Delta_0$ absoluteness and Lemmas 9.2 and 8.4", Lean
does not use $\Delta_0$ absoluteness at all. Since $M,c,U,S$ are already available as external
objects, there is no need to move between the inside and the outside of $L_\theta$. What is
needed instead is the lemma "a recognized axiom is a sentence".

**Direction $\Leftarrow$.** Suppose $\eta$ is admissible.

- Apply (2) of [P-08](P-08.md) to $\theta$ and $\eta < \theta$ and take $M,c \in L_\theta$
  with $\mathrm{LCode}(\eta,M,c)$. This is what "Corollary 10.6" in the paper amounts to.
  Lemma 9.2 gives $M = L_\eta$.
- Apply (1) of [P-08](P-08.md) to $A := L_\eta$ and take $U,S \in L_\theta$ with
  $\mathrm{SatCode}(L_\eta,U,S)$. This is what "the existence of a satisfaction code inside
  $L_\theta$" in the paper amounts to.
- Show $\mathrm{KPTrue}(L_\eta,U,S)$. Let $d \in L_\omega$ with $\mathrm{KPAx}(d)$. Then $d$
  is the code of some $\varphi$ and $\varphi$ is a sentence. Since $\eta$ is admissible, (a)
  of [P-04](P-04.md) gives $L_\eta \models \varphi$. Correctness of the satisfaction code
  gives $\langle d,\emptyset\rangle \in S$.

## Why it could not be written as in the paper

There are two differences, and both come from the medium of the coding.

### (i) The quantifier ranges over $L_\omega$, not $\omega$

The paper's $\mathrm{KPTrue}$ has $\forall d \in \omega$, whereas Lean has $\forall d \in h$
($h = L_\omega$). Since codes are not natural numbers, they cannot be bounded by $\omega$. The
reason is the same as in [P-01](P-01.md).

The range of quantification itself cannot be narrowed. The antecedent of $\forall d \in h$ is
$\mathrm{KPAx}(d)$, that is, "$d$ is the code of the universal closure of a $\mathrm{KP}$
axiom", so the $d$ that satisfy the antecedent are in fact only codes. This agrees with the
paper's $\forall d \in \omega\ (\mathrm{KPAx}(d) \to \cdots)$ both in role and in extension.

### (ii) There are two fixed parameters

The paper's $\mathrm{Adm}$ has $\omega$ as its only parameter, and the support of the
$\hat\Sigma_1$ formula is $\{\omega,\eta\}$. Lean has the two $(h,w) = (L_\omega,\omega)$, and
the support becomes $\{h,w,x\}$. The reason $h$ cannot be removed is the same as in
[P-01](P-01.md).

There is a way to drop one element of the support: absorb $w$ into the leading existential
block of the $\hat\Sigma_1$ formula and add the $\Delta_0$ conjunct "$w$ is $\omega$". It would
take about 60 lines and would need no change upstream. It is not taken because it would add to
the definition of admissibility a conjunct that the paper does not have.

The paper's own conclusion, "$\mathrm{Adm}$ is a single strict $\hat\Sigma_1$ formula", does
hold. That is the only property needed in order to use $\mathrm{Adm}$ as $\hat\Sigma_1$ in the
finite pattern reflection of §17, so there is no effect downstream.
